How It Works

From sustainable forest harvesting to direct digital bank transfer in six transparent steps.

Step 01

Forest Collection

Sustainable harvesting by local tribal women SHGs.

Step 02

Van Kendra Aggregation

Collective deposit at the nearest village center.

Step 03

Quality Check

Digital moisture & purity grading with photo verify.

Step 04

Transparent Pricing

Automated weighment tied directly to published MSP.

Step 05

Market Linkage

Direct supply to buyers, pharma labs & export hubs.

Step 06

Better Income

Instant direct DBT transfer into women's bank accounts.

Institutional Transformation

Women-Led Enterprise Model

Putting technological ownership and financial leadership directly into the hands of tribal women.

Women at the Core

100% of village aggregation centers are operated by local tribal Mahila Mandals and SHG leaders.

Decision Making

Community price committees set aggregation schedules and negotiate corporate off-taker contracts.

Income in Women's Hands

Direct Aadhaar-enabled bank payouts ensure funds reach women directly, fueling health and schooling.

Capacity Building

Hands-on training in digital weighing, solar drying, grading, and organic certifications.

Ownership & Dignity

Transforming seasonal gatherers into respected rural co-owners and environmental forest stewards.

Scalability & Expansion Roadmap

From pilot blocks to nationwide Van Dhan ecosystem coverage

Phase 1 (Current)

Expand Locally

Deploy voice kiosks in 25 pilot Van Kendras across Khunti, Torpa, and Murhu blocks in Jharkhand.

Phase 2 (Q4 2026)

Scale Across Districts

Integrate 24 districts across Jharkhand, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh reaching 50,000+ collectors.

Phase 3 (2027)

Build Partnerships

Direct B2B supply contracts with leading Ayurvedic manufacturers, organic retailers, and TRIFED export desks.

Phase 4 (2028)

Go National

Pan-India rollout supporting 30+ tribal dialects and connecting 5,000+ Van Dhan Vikas Kendra Clusters.

Digital Inclusivity

Technology & Innovation

Cutting-edge voice AI and decentralized pricing engines engineered specifically for low-connectivity forest villages.
